## The question

The technique behind a trick happens in a fraction of a second. A skater who films themselves can
see that an attempt failed, but not why. I wanted to know whether the board itself could measure
every attempt and turn that into feedback for the next one, without a coach standing next to you.

## What I built

- **A sensor module.** An ESP32 reads an MPU6050 (acceleration and rotation) and four
  force-sensitive resistors under the grip tape: nose, heel, toe and tail. It runs on a LiPo with a
  charger and boost converter, inside a PETG enclosure mounted between the trucks.
- **Firmware.** Reads the sensors at around 100 Hz, classifies tricks with rules, and streams
  compact JSON over Bluetooth Low Energy.
- **A mobile app.** React Native with Expo: sign-in, board pairing, a live dashboard (pitch, roll,
  impact, foot pressure), live sessions with a trick feed, session summaries, a learning and
  practice flow, and history stored in Firestore.
- **AI coaching.** After a trick or a session, the app turns the measurements into short coaching
  cues with the Claude API.

## How it works

When the board's rotation rises above 70°/s, the firmware opens a short window. It records the peak
rotation on each axis until the board settles or 480 ms pass, then checks the peaks against fixed
thresholds:

- **Flip:** rotation around the length axis of at least 220°/s, and at least 1.35× the pop axis.
  The direction decides kickflip or heelflip.
- **Shove-it:** vertical rotation of at least 170°/s, dominant over the other axes. The direction
  decides backside or frontside.
- **Ollie:** pop-axis rotation of at least 150°/s.

## How I tested it

I tested the hardware and app on the bench with the PlatformIO serial monitor and an Expo
development build on Android, then in a real skate session with the live dashboard. Along the way I
fixed pressure-sensor cross-talk, a sensor bus that dropped out under vibration (by slowing I²C to
100 kHz) and a stale closure that froze the pitch and roll readouts. There is no automated test
suite yet.
